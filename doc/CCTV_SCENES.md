# Editing observation feeds

Open `scenes/cctv/locations/office.tscn`, `observation_shop.tscn`, or `street.tscn` in Godot. These are the **current shift** locations. `shop.tscn` and `yard.tscn` are separate prototype/menu locations, not the shift's office/shop/street.

- Build and move the level directly in the scene editor. The root uses `ObservationLocation` (with a small scene-specific script for evidence/events). `Target` is the moving subject. Move the subject's starting position in the scene.
- Drag `scenes/cctv/cctv_camera.tscn` anywhere under the location root, position and rotate it in 3D. It appears automatically in the PC; configure `display_name`, `location_label` and `list_order` in Inspector. Empty `camera_id` gets an automatic path-based ID; set an explicit **unique** ID if it must stay stable when renaming/moving nodes. Duplicate IDs emit a warning and are skipped. Only the selected camera is rendered in the shared 480×270 viewport. The camera looks down its **negative Z** axis; edit its `Head/Camera3D` FOV for editor preview, and `base_fov` for runtime.
- Under `Route`, add/duplicate `Marker3D` nodes with `observation_waypoint.gd`. Their **tree order** is chronological; edit their position, `seconds` and optional `event_id` in Inspector. The subject moves between these nodes even when the PC/feed is closed. Office currently has three points; shop has seven (`take` and `checkout` events); street has a `branch` event and three destination markers under `Exits` (tree order = alley, avenue, park).
- The office screen code is written onto `Screen/Code`; shop purchase objects live under `Drinks` and are referenced by `drink_paths` on its root. Keep those nodes assigned if changing evidence props. Street branch camera destinations follow `Exits` markers; when moving a street exit, also move its corresponding camera and visual path. For an entirely new type of event, add its behavior to the location script rather than the CCTV UI.
- The shift scene list is editable in Inspector on `scenes/cctv/observation_shift.tscn` (`ShiftDirector.stages`). Each entry is a `PackedScene` with an `ObservationLocation` root. The final entry ends at the report. The current quiz still expects this three-stage office/shop/street story; adding a new **story stage**, as opposed to adding cameras or props, also needs story/quiz review.

Validation: run `godot --headless --path . --script res://tests/shift_smoke.gd` and `res://tests/cctv_smoke.gd`; then check the camera views in the editor/game. Web exports should be checked in a browser after visual changes.
